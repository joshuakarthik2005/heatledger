HeatLedger Verify

Buyer-side check of a receipt chain · runs entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded · ← Back to Studio

1. Paste the receipt chain

A JSON array of {"payload": "...", "hash": "..."}, one entry per heat, oldest first. The foundry produces it with Export chain JSON on the Studio's Receipts tab.

2. Paste the anchored head hash

The head (last) hash as it was posted outside the foundry at the end of the shift, from your own record, not from the foundry's copy of the chain.

Result

OK means: every heat's hash matches its content, every heat links to the one before, and the last hash equals the anchor you supplied. It proves the record was not changed after the anchor was posted. It does not prove the meter was correct or that the readings were honest at the time.
