Model assumptions

Your heat log

Columns: heat, grade, weight_kg, start, tap, kwh, remarks. Common header variants are recognised (for example "Heat No.", "Wt (kg)", "Tap time", "kWh (meter)", "Notes"); if a required column is not recognised you will be asked to map it. Grade and remarks are optional. If kwh is blank, meter_after − meter_before is used. Comma, semicolon and tab-separated text (including cells pasted from a spreadsheet) all work; weights are kilograms unless the header says tonnes. Times are HH:MM: a tap earlier than the start, or a start earlier than the previous heat's start, is read as crossing midnight. Remarks containing "hold" or "long" flag a heat. Parsed in your browser only.

Results

Meter series

CSV columns: timestamp,kw. Timestamp is ISO (2026-01-31T06:00) or HH:MM; 1-minute data is best, 5-minute data is accepted with reduced accuracy. kW values are never clipped.

Detected heats

Enter tonnes per heat, or match to the heat log from the other mode. Leave tonnes blank to exclude a detection (for example a false positive).
